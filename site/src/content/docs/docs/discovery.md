---
title: Nearby devices
description: Find other Truedesk computers on the same local network and connect by name, without typing an ID.
---

Truedesk finds other Truedesk computers on the same local network and lists
them on the home screen, so you can connect by name instead of typing a
nine-digit ID.

## Connect to a nearby device

Under **Control a remote device**, an **On this network** row shows each
Truedesk computer discovered on your network, by its name. Click one to fill
in its ID, then enter that computer's password and connect.

The list refreshes every few seconds: a computer appears a moment after
Truedesk starts on it and drops off a few seconds after it stops.

## What is shared

While Truedesk is running it broadcasts only its **ID** and its **device
name** (the computer's hostname) on the local network, so other Truedesk
computers can offer it in their list. It never broadcasts your screen,
password or any session data, and nothing leaves the local network.

Discovery is only a shortcut for the connect screen: you still need the other
computer's password, and its approval unless it has unattended access, to
start a session.
